# Prize stacking rules: PayPal AI Hackathon

Source: https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/rules
Fetched: 2026-11-03 (via curl; WebFetch returned HTTP 403). Quotes are verbatim from the page text.

## (a) Grand Prize vs Honorable Mention, and (b) number of Sponsor Prizes per project

Section 8. Prizes, "IMPORTANT NOTES ON MULTIPLE PRIZE ELIGIBILITY":

> A project can only win up to one (1) Grand Prize and one (1) Sponsor Prize OR one (1) Honorable Mention Prize and one (1) Sponsor Prize.

This one sentence covers both (a) and (b).

- Grand Prize and Honorable Mention are mutually exclusive ("OR").
- A project can win at most one Sponsor Prize alongside either one.

## (c) One entrant winning in multiple sponsor categories

No separate clause addresses this directly. Two relevant passages:

Section 8 note above: "one (1) Sponsor Prize" applies per project, whichever sponsor category it comes from.

Submission requirements, multiple submissions:

> An Entrant may submit more than one Submission, however, each Submission must be unique and substantially different from each of the Entrant's other Submissions, as determined by the Sponsor and Devpost in their sole discretion.

The cap is worded per project, not per entrant. A separate, substantially different Submission could in principle win its own prizes. That depends on the Sponsor's and Devpost's sole-discretion judgment of "unique and substantially different".

## Sponsor Prize requirements

> Sponsor Prizes: Projects being submitted for Sponsor Prizes, must also follow the specific requirements listed in section 8. Prizes.

Sponsor categories listed: AG Grid (1st/2nd/3rd), APIMatic, Bryntum, Channel3, Render (1st/2nd/3rd). Each is open to "All Eligible Submissions that use any [X] tools".
